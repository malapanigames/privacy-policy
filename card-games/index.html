Privacy Policy

Card Games by Malapani Games · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the mobile game Card Games (the "Game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The Game is made by Malapani Games ("we", "us"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game does not ask you to create an account. The following is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off:

  • the player name and avatar you choose,
  • game settings (sound, music, language, difficulty and house rules),
  • statistics and saved matches.

We cannot see or access this saved information.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads to stay free. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks it works with, including Unity Ads. To show, limit and measure ads, these services may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • device details such as model, operating system, language and screen size,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

This information is collected and used by those providers under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads) privacy policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK or some US states), you may be asked for your consent to personalised ads, and you can refuse.

3. Analytics

To understand how the Game is used and to improve it, the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It sends us usage information that does not include your name or contact details, such as:

  • which games you open and when you start and finish a match,
  • match results (win, loss, draw or abandoned), number of players and difficulty,
  • general app usage, such as sessions and how long the Game is used,
  • device details such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • an app instance identifier and, where available, your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address.

We look at this information in summary form, for example how many players play Rummy, to decide what to improve and which games to add. We do not use it to identify you. Google's handling of this information is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases in the Game are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything the Game saved on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us (section 9). For data held by Unity or Google, you can also use the options in their privacy policies above.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

7. Security

Your saved games and settings stay on your device. Analytics, advertising and payment information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by Google and Unity as described in their privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please email malapanigames@gmail.com.
